Case study / SaaS & dashboard design

CRMFrom spreadsheets to one sales flow.

An IT services company ran its sales on Excel and Google Sheets. When the team grew, the sheets stopped working, so they built their own CRM, and we designed it.

ClientIT services company NDA
ProductInternal CRM & admin panel
UsersSales team, agents & admins
ServicesProduct design, UX flows, UI in Figma
BeforeExcel & Google Sheets
NoteBrand and real data hidden
01 — The problem

The team grew,the sheets didn't.

Leads, follow-ups and quotes lived in Excel and Google Sheets. That works for a few people. With a bigger team, nobody had the full picture of a lead.

01

Leads spread across sheets

Each person kept their own tab, so the same lead could sit in two places with different details, and no one knew who owned it.

02

Follow-ups depended on memory

A spreadsheet can't remind anyone. If a sales rep forgot the call, the lead quietly went cold.

03

Quotes and GST done by hand

Discounts and GST were worked out with formulas or a calculator, which is slow and easy to get wrong.

04

No clear outcome

There was no single place that said whether a lead was won, lost or on hold, or at what final price.

02 — The approach

Design the waya lead moves.

Instead of one big form, we split a lead into the six steps a deal actually goes through. Every lead follows the same path, and every step asks only for what it needs.

01

Lead

Owner, agent, source, project and status.

02

Description

What the client needs, in their words.

03

Follow up

Date, time and notes for the next call.

04

Client info

Contact and company details.

05

Billing

Billing type, GST, discount and total.

06

Note

Converted, not converted or on hold.

03 — The solution

Problem byproblem.

Problem 01Leads spread across sheets

One lead, one owner.

Every lead starts with the same fields: a lead owner, the agents on it, where it came from, the project type and its status. The team stops guessing who is handling what.

  • Lead owner and assigned agents
  • Lead sources managed by admins
  • A status on every lead
Problem 02Follow-ups depended on memory

The next call, on the calendar.

Follow-ups get a real date and time, with notes from the last conversation. A lead can have several follow-ups, so the whole history stays with the lead instead of in someone's head.

  • Date and time picker for each follow-up
  • Notes with every follow-up
  • Add as many follow-ups as a deal needs
Problem 03Quotes and GST done by hand

The maths, done for you.

Pick a billing type, choose GST or non-GST, add a discount, and the subtotal, tax and total update on their own. No formulas and no calculator.

  • Cash or account transfer billing
  • GST and non-GST options
  • Automatic subtotal, discount and total
Problem 04No clear outcome

Every lead ends somewhere.

The last step closes the loop. Each lead is marked converted, not converted or on hold, with the closing agent, the final price, GST and any files, all in one record.

  • Three clear outcomes for every lead
  • Final price and GST on record
  • Upload files like proposals and contracts

Six steps,one record.

The full lead flow, designed in Figma. Client name and real data are hidden.

04 — The result

Out of thespreadsheets.

The team got a CRM made for how they sell: every lead, follow-up and bill in one place, designed to replace the shared sheets.

6steps from first contact to a closed deal
1record per lead, with its follow-ups, billing and outcome
3clear outcomes: converted, not converted or on hold
