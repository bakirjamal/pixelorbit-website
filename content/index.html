FAQ

Goodquestions.

Anything else, ask on the free call or send us a message.

01

How much does a project cost?

It depends on the pages, features and platforms. After a free 30-minute call we send a written scope with a fixed price, so you know the cost before any work starts.

02

How long does it take?

The timeline is written into the scope. Websites usually move faster than apps and SaaS products, and you approve each step before the next one begins.

03

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. We work with clients in the UK, US, Australia and Europe by video call, email and WhatsApp, with regular updates in your time zone.

04

Which technologies do you use?

MERN for the web, React Native, Flutter or native for mobile, and custom builds or Shopify for e-commerce. Design happens in Figma.

05

What happens after launch?

Every project includes 60 days of free support after launch. After that we can keep looking after it on a care plan.

06

Will you sign an NDA?

Yes. Several of our projects are under NDA, which is why some of our work is shown without the client's name.
