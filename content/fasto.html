Case study 01 / Mobile apps, admin panel & website

FastoFrom WhatsApp chats to a real delivery app.

Fasto delivered groceries and stationery to students in Delhi, and every order came in over WhatsApp. We designed and built the full product in about two months.

IndustryGrocery & stationery delivery
UsersStudents
ServicesProduct design, React Native, admin panel, website
Team & time3 people, about 2 months

02 — The goal

Every order,one system.

Move every order out of WhatsApp and into one connected product for students, delivery partners and the Fasto team.

01

Customer app

Browse, order, pay and track, for students.

02

Delivery partner app

Orders and addresses for the riders.

03

Admin panel

Orders, products and stock for the Fasto team.

04

Website

The public home of the brand at fastoapp.com.

Designed in Figma and built in React Native by a Pixelorbits team of 3, in about two months.

03 — The solution

Problem byproblem.

Problem 01Orders got missed

A cart that never forgets.

Students add items from a clear home screen and check out in a few taps. Every order gets an order summary and an order ID, so nothing gets lost in a chat.

  • Home with offers, trending items and categories
  • Cart and checkout with an order summary
  • Coupons applied at checkout
Problem 02Payments were hard to track

Every payment on record.

Payments moved into the app. Students sign in with their phone number and pay with UPI apps, cards, wallets or net banking, and every payment is tied to an order.

  • Phone number sign-in with OTP
  • UPI, cards, wallets and net banking
  • Fasto wallet with transaction history
Problem 03Addresses were unclear

A pin, not a paragraph.

Students drop a pin on the map and save their addresses, so the delivery partner gets the exact location. The student then follows the order live, with the partner's name and a call button.

  • Address search and pin on the map
  • Saved addresses like Home and Office
  • Live tracking with arrival countdown
ExtraDaily essentials

Milk, on repeat.

For things students buy every day, like milk and bread, we added subscriptions. Pick a plan, choose a start date, and pause it when going home for the holidays.

  • Daily plans with a start date
  • Subscription status by day
  • Vacation mode to pause deliveries

40+ screens,one system.

From sign-in to support, every screen was designed in Figma and built in React Native.

04 — The result

Off WhatsApp,for good.

After launch, every order came through the app. Fasto stopped taking orders on WhatsApp.
