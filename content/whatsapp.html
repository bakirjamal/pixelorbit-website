Case study / SaaS design & development

WhatsAppFrom one phone to a whole team.

A business messaging company wanted a platform on the official WhatsApp Business API, so teams could handle customer chats, campaigns and automation in one place. We designed and built it in-house.

ProductWeb dashboard (SaaS)

01 — The problem

Every customeron one phone.

Businesses get most of their customer messages on WhatsApp, but often on a single phone. The more customers they had, the more chats were missed.

01

One phone, many people

Only the person holding the phone could reply, and nobody knew who was handling which chat.

02

Bulk messages got numbers blocked

Sending offers to many customers from a normal account risked getting the number banned.

03

The same questions, all day

Order status, prices and timings were answered by hand, again and again.

04

No idea what worked

There was no record of how many messages were read or answered.

02 — The approach

Turn a phoneinto a team tool.

We built the platform around four tools that match a team's day: a shared inbox, broadcasts, a chatbot and reports, all on the official API.
