Case study / E-commerce design & development

FashionA store that sells like the brand looks.

A growing fashion label was selling through social media DMs and a template store that didn't feel like the brand. We designed and built a custom e-commerce store in-house.

01 — The problem

A great brand,a generic store.

The label had loyal followers on Instagram, but its online store looked like every other template. Shoppers browsed, then left before paying.

01

The store didn't feel like the brand

A stock template made the clothes look ordinary and the brand forgettable.

02

Hard to find the right piece

Shoppers had to scroll through everything with no useful filters.

03

Sizing doubts

Unsure of fit, shoppers messaged on Instagram or simply didn't buy.

04

A long checkout

Too many steps and few payment options lost people at the last moment.

02 — The approach

Let the clothesdo the talking.

We designed an editorial store with big imagery and quiet UI, and made every step from browsing to paying shorter.
