Case study / Web app design & development

AudioA home for voices on the internet.

A media startup wanted a place where anyone could record a story, a talk or a poem and share it in seconds. We designed and built the web app together with our partner studio.

ClientAudio creator platform NDA
ProductMobile-first web app
UsersAudio creators & listeners

NoteVisuals are illustrative

01 — The problem

Great voices,nowhere to live.

Short audio lived in voice notes, podcast apps and social posts. Creators had no simple place to record, keep and share it.

01

Recording felt technical

Most tools expected a mic setup, an editing app and an export step before anything could be shared.

02

Audio got lost in chats

Voice notes were shared once in a group chat and then disappeared in the scroll.

03

Hard to be discovered

New creators had no feed where listeners could find them by topic.

04

Sharing audio is awkward

A link to an audio file shows nothing, so few people click it.

02 — The approach

Press record,then share.

We kept the core loop to three steps and made it work in the phone browser, so there is no app to install before the first recording.

01

Record or upload

Straight from the phone browser.

02

Publish to a profile

Every creator gets a public page.

03

Explore

A feed by topic for listeners.

04

Share

Links that show a playable preview.
