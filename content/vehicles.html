Case study / Mobile app design & development

VehiclesEvery kind of vehicle, one marketplace.

A global marketplace wanted one app for buying and selling pre-owned vehicles, from cars and bikes to boats, buses and heavy equipment. We designed and built it in-house.

01 — The problem

Many vehicles,one messy search.

Buyers and sellers of anything beyond a car had to jump between classifieds, groups and dealers. Nothing was built for every kind of vehicle.

01

Niche vehicles had no home

Boats, buses and machinery were buried in general classified sites.

02

One filter for everything

The details that matter for a boat are not the ones that matter for a bike.

03

Listings with missing details

Buyers couldn't trust a listing without photos, year or condition.

04

Selling took too long

Posting a vehicle meant long forms that many sellers gave up on.

02 — The approach

Start withthe category.

The whole app starts from the type of vehicle. Pick a category, and the filters, listing details and sell form all adapt to it.
